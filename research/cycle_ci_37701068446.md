## Research

## Implementation

- `can_rotate_to` requires obtained/goal/796/can-be-rotated; excludes "is a rotation of another"
- `second_largest` accepts second largest/highest, still excludes digit/1796
- `left_pad` / `right_pad` accept pad-left / pad-right
- `group_consecutive` accepts group+consecutive without requiring "list"
